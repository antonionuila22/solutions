## What a US business website needs today

- **Accessibility.** Accessibility claims against business websites are common in the US. We build with the WCAG guidelines in mind so the site works for every visitor and stands up to review.
- **Privacy that matches state law.** California and a growing list of states regulate how websites collect personal data. Consent, privacy policy and data handling are designed in, not bolted on.
- **Sales tax and payments.** Online stores charge sales tax where the business has nexus, and checkout has to support cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal through Stripe or a similar processor.
- **Integrations with the tools you already use.** CRM, scheduling, email marketing and accounting, connected so leads and orders do not get typed in twice.
- **Speed and technical SEO.** A fast, well structured site ranks and converts better than a heavier one with the same design.
