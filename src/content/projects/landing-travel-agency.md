## Context

TravelUp is a travel agency in Honduras with two offices, San Pedro Sula and Tegucigalpa. They sell nine different service lines — flights, accommodation, all-inclusive packages, transfers, car rental, guided tours, travel insurance, cruises and group travel — and work with carriers including Spirit, Delta, Avianca, Copa and American Airlines.

## The challenge

Every reservation was handled by hand. Phone calls, WhatsApp threads and spreadsheets, with an agent re-typing the same passenger details into each one.

That created two commercial problems. The first was capacity: agent time went into data capture instead of selling, so the volume the agency could handle was capped by how fast people could type. The second was visibility. Travellers research trips at night and on weekends, and they research on Google — where competitors appeared and TravelUp did not. An enquiry that arrived outside office hours simply went to whoever answered first.

## How we worked

**1. Service inventory.** We mapped all nine service lines and how the agency actually prices each one, so the site could reflect the real business instead of a generic "travel packages" page.

**2. A booking form that captures what an agent needs.** Name, phone, email, city of origin, destination, entry and exit dates, number of adults and children — plus two fields specific to how travel is billed in Honduras: whether the buyer is a legal entity and whether they are tax-exempt. Those are exactly the fields required to price a trip, so a request arrives ready to quote rather than starting another round of questions.

**3. WhatsApp built into the flow, not bolted on.** WhatsApp is how Honduras books travel. We integrated it as a first-class channel alongside the form, instead of treating it as an afterthought.

**4. Deals with the price on them.** Six featured destinations plus a flash-deals block where each departure carries its price, so a traveller can self-qualify on budget before contacting anyone.

**5. Local SEO.** Structure and content aimed at how Hondurans actually search for flights and packages.

**6. Both offices on one funnel.** San Pedro Sula and Tegucigalpa work from the same intake, with the agency's contact channels published on every page.

## Results

- **10 → 20 daily clients** — figure reported by TravelUp after the October 2024 launch. We did not measure it and cannot verify it; it is published here as their number, not ours.
- **9 travel service lines published online**, each one explained, where previously none of them were.
- **6 flash deals with the price printed on them**, letting travellers qualify themselves on budget before they contact an agent.
- **Requests arrive pre-qualified**, with dates and passenger counts already captured, instead of starting from a blank WhatsApp message.
- **2 offices operating from a single online intake**, available outside business hours.

Everything except the first bullet is still live and checkable on agenciatraveluphn.com.

## Tech stack

WordPress with Elementor on the hello-elementor theme, an Elementor form for booking requests, WhatsApp click-to-chat, Google Site Kit, on-page SEO and a responsive front-end.

## What we delivered

- Online reservation request flow with a quote-ready booking form
- WhatsApp integration as a primary contact channel
- Featured destination showcase across six destinations
- Flash deals section with the price printed on each departure
- Carrier logos (Spirit, Delta, Avianca, Copa, American Airlines) and a Google reviews block
- Local SEO optimisation for Honduran search
- Mobile-optimised experience for travellers browsing on the go
- Contact routing for both the San Pedro Sula and Tegucigalpa offices
