## How to Maximize Savings with Honduras Development

### **Tip 1: Bundle Projects**
Doing multiple projects? Scoping them together lets one team reuse the same design system, backend and infrastructure, so the combined proposal covers less work than two separate ones.

### **Tip 2: Provide Assets**
Have your own designs, copy and photography ready? Design hours drop out of the scope and go into features instead.

### **Tip 3: Flexible Timeline**
Not in a rush? A delivery window without hard deadlines lets us plan the team more efficiently across weeks.

### **Tip 4: Start Small**
Begin with an **MVP**, then expand once it is earning. A smaller first scope costs less upfront, and what you learn from the first release shapes the second.

### **Tip 5: Milestone Payments**
Pay per milestone rather than all upfront. Spreading the payments across the build keeps your cash flow healthier while the work is in progress.
