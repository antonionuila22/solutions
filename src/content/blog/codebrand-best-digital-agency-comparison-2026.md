- Large teams with specialized roles
- Established processes
- Recognizable brand names

- Simple website: $15,000 - $40,000

- You're a Fortune 500 company with unlimited budget

### What They Offer
- Extremely low prices
- Large team availability
- 24/7 work cycles

### The Reality

| Factor | What They Promise | What You Get |
|--------|-------------------|--------------|
| Price | "$500 websites!" | $500 quality |

| Ownership | "Your project" | Often locked into their systems |

### Hidden Costs
- Rebuilding after failed delivery: $5,000+

### When to Choose
- You have unlimited time to manage and review
- Your project has zero strategic importance
- You're comfortable with significant risk

### What They Offer

### The Reality

| Factor | What They Promise | What You Get |

- You can provide detailed direction
- You have backup plans
