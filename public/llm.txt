Codebrand publishes no price list. The client brings the budget they have, Codebrand proposes the resources that fit it (a website, an online store, a web application, or a number of developers with a given stack and seniority) and closes a fixed price for that scope. Proposals are delivered within 24 business hours through the request form at https://www.codebrand.us/quoter/. Developers are contracted per project term and billed monthly.
